[size=6][b]Damage Destruction[/b][/size]
[i]Revises death, destruction and resurrection mechanics.[/i]

How you die now matters. Lethal damage can destroy the gear you were wearing, some deaths can't be undone with a quick Revivify, corpses become harder to raise with every long rest, and bringing someone back has a price. On top of that, this mod repairs the story state of NPCs and companions you revive, so they behave as if they'd survived instead of coming back mute or broken.

Damage Destruction includes every fix from [b]Raise The Dead[/b]. [color=#ff6666][b]Don't install both together.[/b][/color]

[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]BG3 Script Extender
[*]Mod Configuration Menu (MCM)
[*]5eSpells and ImmersiveNudity (listed as dependencies)
[/list]

[size=5][b]Features[/b][/size]

[size=4][b]Item destruction on death[/b][/size]
The killing blow's damage type decides what happens to the victim's equipped gear. Each damage type can be set to destroy [b]all[/b], [b]random[/b] or [b]no[/b] items.
[list]
[*][b]Defaults:[/b] Acid, Fire, Cold, Lightning, Radiant and Necrotic destroy everything. Physical and other damage types destroy random items.
[*][b]Overkill tiers:[/b] damage beyond the victim's remaining HP destroys more items, one extra item per 10 overkill damage by default. This is configurable per damage type.
[*][b]Slashing:[/b] in random mode, Slashing only cuts away the chest item.
[*][b]Who it affects:[/b] the whole world or just your party.
[*][b]Immunities:[/b] Legendary and Adamantine items are immune by default, and you can add your own immune items.
[*][b]Notifications:[/b] a notification names the character, the item and the cause for every destroyed item.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Equipment failure in combat[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Hits:[/b] a hit has a 5% chance to break an item, and a crit has a separate 50% chance.
[*][b]Downed:[/b] going down has a 50% chance to break an item.
[*][b]Saves:[/b] each break is followed by a Dexterity save (DC 10) to keep the item.
[*][b]Burning and Acid:[/b] these conditions can also break gear. The save uses the better of Dex or Con, with optional advantage.
[/list]
All values are configurable.

[size=4][b]Ash-body deaths, Disintegrate and exploding corpses[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Ash-body deaths:[/b] dying to Disintegrate, or shattering after being petrified, turns the body to ash. It blocks Raise Dead and, optionally, destroys all the gear they wore.
[*][b]Disintegrate saves:[/b] surviving a Disintegrate save still destroys one random item (optional).
[*][b]Exploding corpses:[/b] optional for Bludgeoning, Force and Thunder. Big enough lethal hits make the corpse explode, and an exploded corpse can't be raised normally.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Death that sticks[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Recent-death window:[/b] the dead have about 10 rounds during which Revivify still works.
[*][b]Elemental permadeath:[/b] Fire, Acid, Lightning, Cold, Radiant and Necrotic killing blows can apply the strongest Raise Dead block immediately. Choose on any death, only on overkill, or off.
[*][b]Long-rest ladder:[/b] every long rest makes a corpse harder to raise. Raise Dead needs a higher spell slot each time, until only a 9th-level Raise Dead, a True Resurrection scroll or Withers can bring them back.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Resurrection has a cost[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Constitution penalty:[/b] Resurrection and True Resurrection scrolls give both caster and target a stacking Constitution penalty, up to -8.
[*][b]Withers is the exception:[/b] he's exempt from the penalty, and being resurrected by him clears it.
[*][b]Withers' price:[/b] his resurrection fee rises to 8,500 gold.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Empty slots grant initiative[/b][/size]
+1 initiative for each empty weapon, armour, helmet and cloak slot. Travelling light pays off.

[size=4][b]Dead Party Members tab (MCM)[/b][/size]
This tab lists your dead party members and offers two ways to deal with them, so you don't have to wait for Withers:
[list]
[*][b]Send to Camp:[/b] the same as Withers' dismissal.
[*][b]Leave Body, Remove:[/b] takes them out of the party where they died.
[/list]
Anyone removed this way [b]rejoins your party automatically when revived[/b], even if your party is full.

[size=4][b]Avatar Switching tab (MCM), experimental[/b][/size]
While your custom character is dead, you can make a living origin companion your main character, then hand control back when your character is revived. [i]Keep a save before you use it.[/i]

[size=4][b]Halsin's gear restore[/b][/size]
If Halsin dies before recruitment and permadeath destroys his gear, his equipment goes back on him when he's revived.

[size=5][b]NPCs accounted for[/b][/size]

[b]Every revived character gets:[/b]
[list]
[*]Their leftover "defeated / can't talk" story state cleared.
[*]The "stolen goods" overlay removed from their body and gear.
[*]Talking working straight away. No more "not right now" until a long rest.
[*]Unlisted NPCs who are still broken after revival are respawned in place.
[/list]

[b]Characters with dedicated fixes:[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Baelen Bonecloak[/b] (Act 1, Underdark). His full "rescued" quest state and dialogue are restored, and he goes back to Derryth.
[*][b]Scratch[/b] (Act 1). Gets the right dialogue for how far his quest had gone.
[*][b]Oskar Fevros[/b] (Act 1, Zhentarim hideout). His dialogue and "freed" state are restored, so his Act 3 quest stays available.
[*][b]Arabella[/b] (Act 1, Druid Grove). Gets the "Kagha talked down" outcome and is reunited with her parents. Rath stops mourning her, and her dialogue is restored. [i]Confirmed in play.[/i]
[*][b]Aradin[/b] (Act 1, Grove entrance). His dialogue is restored to match how his confrontation with Zevlor ended.
[*][b]Minthara[/b] (Act 1, Grove attack, before Grymforge or Moonrise). Returns to the game's own "spared" state, so she continues to Moonrise as normal.
[*][b]Mirkon[/b] (Act 1, harpy beach). His death flags and charm are removed. If the harpies are dead, he gets the "saved the victim" outcome.
[*][b]Halsin[/b] (Act 1, goblin camp). If the goblin leaders are dead, he thanks you and heads to the Grove. If they're alive, he follows you again.
[*][b]Damon[/b] (Act 1, Druid Grove). Rejoins the Druid Attack fight correctly. Karlach's "Damon is dead" memory is cleared, so he can still upgrade her. [i]Confirmed in play.[/i]
[*][b]Eremus[/b] (Act 1, Druid Grove). Rejoins the Druid Attack fight correctly. [i]Confirmed in play.[/i]
[/list]

[b]Known limits:[/b] a journal entry that has already been written (for example "X was defeated") can't be erased. A quest the game closed because of a death can't be reopened, but the character still works normally. Fixes are added one character at a time, because BG3 scripts every story NPC by hand. Requests are welcome.

[size=4][b]Compatibility[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Configurable Party Limit:[/b] works with it.
[*][b]ImmersiveNudity SE Patch:[/b] destroyed gear is also stripped visually.
[*][b]Other mods:[/b] mods that change Revivify, Raise Dead, Resurrection or Withers may conflict.
[/list]
